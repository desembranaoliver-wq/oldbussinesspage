Hi! I'm a Filipino Computer Science student who loves creating digital art using just my phone and my fingers. I mostly focus on portraits, where I try to capture emotion, character, and subtle details through simple yet expressive strokes.

This online portfolio is actually my final project for my 3rd year, first semester. A blend of my passion for art and my course in tech. It's both a showcase of my works and a personal reminder that creativity can thrive anywhere, even with limited tools. Thanks for visiting, and I hope my art inspires you as much as I enjoyed making it!
